Build your first AI agent, two ways

Both paths begin the same way: open the website and sign in. Then you choose. Viber asks a few questions and builds the agent for you. Studio gives you a visual canvas to build it yourself. Every step below shows the real screen and what to do on it.

Step 0 · The same for both paths

Open the website and sign in

  1. 1Open your browser and go to botpress.com. The agent builder lives there.
  2. 2Click Log in, or Sign up if you are new, and finish signing in. The button names may vary slightly.
  3. 3You land in your own workspace. The free plan is enough for this walkthrough.
  4. 4From here, choose Path A (Viber) or Path B (Studio) below.

Connect it to the six AI words

What you just built, in the earlier vocabulary

Look again at the Studio canvas. The ideas from the explainer page are right there on screen. (Open the six-word explainer)

AI agentThe Autonomous node

An AI step that follows written instructions and uses tools such as Search Knowledge.

Harness engineeringInstructions and sources

"Only answer from the knowledge base" and "never invent policies" are rules around the model.

Loop engineeringThe Loop tag

The node is labelled Loop: it can think, use a tool and look at the result again.

Graph engineeringStart leads to a node

The canvas is a map of steps. Add more nodes and you are drawing a bigger graph.

Good to know. These screens come from a live product, so button labels and layouts can change. The numbers on the screenshots match the numbered actions beside them. Percentages shown in the Viber setup, such as "80% resolved", are examples on the screen, not results you should expect. Always check an answer against your original document before you publish.
